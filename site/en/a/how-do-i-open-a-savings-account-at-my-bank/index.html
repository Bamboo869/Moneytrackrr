How do I open a savings account at my bank?

25 October 2026·2 min read·Money Trackrr editorial team

A savings account is the simplest home for an emergency fund and money saved for a goal. Here is how it differs from a current account and a term deposit, what to look for in an offer and how to open one in a few minutes.

Keeping all your money in one account has two downsides: current accounts usually pay no interest, and your savings get mixed up with everyday money, so they're easy to spend. A savings account solves both.

What is a savings account?

It's a separate account, usually linked to your current account at the same bank. The money in it earns interest – the bank adds it, most often monthly. Unlike a term deposit, you can withdraw money at any time without losing interest for the whole period.

Compared:

  • current account – for everyday payments, usually no interest,
  • savings account – interest plus access to your money, sometimes with a limit on free withdrawals,
  • term deposit – usually a higher rate, but your money is locked for a set period.

How to open one

At most banks it takes a few minutes in the app:

  1. Log in to your banking app or online banking.
  2. Look for a tab like "Savings", "Products", "Open a new product" or "Accounts".
  3. Choose a savings account and read the terms (fee table and interest rate).
  4. Accept the agreement – usually with an SMS code or in the app.
  5. Done – the new account appears next to your current one and you can make your first transfer straight away.

The exact path depends on your bank. If you can't find it, search the app for "savings" or ask the bank's chat.

What to look for

  • The standard rate, not just the promo rate. A high rate often applies only for a few months or up to a set amount. Check what you'll get afterwards.
  • Promo conditions – e.g. your salary must be paid in, you must use the card, or only "new money" counts.
  • Free withdrawal limit – some accounts charge for the second or later withdrawal in a month.
  • Compounding – the more often interest is added, the better.
  • Safety – in Poland, money in banks is protected by the Bank Guarantee Fund up to the equivalent of EUR 100,000 per person per bank.

Using it wisely

  • Set up a standing order on payday – even PLN 100–200.
  • Name the account after your goal, e.g. "Safety net" or "Holiday" – it's easier not to touch it.
  • When the promo ends, compare offers – moving savings to another bank usually takes a few minutes.

In short

  • Savings account = interest plus access to your money at any time.
  • You can open one in a few minutes in your bank's app.
  • Check the rate after the promo, the conditions and withdrawal fees.
