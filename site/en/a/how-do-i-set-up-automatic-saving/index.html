How do I set up automatic saving?

25 October 2026·2 min read·Money Trackrr editorial team

The most effective saving is the kind you don't have to remember. Here are three ways to put it on autopilot – a standing order, round-ups and "pay yourself first" – plus how to choose an amount that still leaves enough to live on.

Most people save "whatever is left at the end of the month". The problem is that usually nothing is left. Automatic saving flips the order: savings first, spending second. You make the decision once and the bank does the rest.

Method 1: a standing order on payday

This is the foundation. In your banking app you set up a standing order from your current account to your savings account:

  1. Go to transfers and choose "standing order" (sometimes "recurring transfer").
  2. Select your savings account as the recipient.
  3. Set the amount and frequency – monthly.
  4. Pick a date a day or two after payday, so the money is definitely there.

From then on you save without effort. That money never even sits in your spending account.

Method 2: round-ups

Many banks offer a feature that rounds up every card payment (e.g. to the nearest PLN 5 or 10) and moves the difference to savings. A coffee for PLN 13.50? PLN 1.50 goes into the piggy bank.

Each one is pocket change, but with dozens of payments a month it adds up to a real amount – without you feeling it. Look in your app for something called "piggy bank", "round-ups" or "spare change".

Method 3: separate accounts for separate goals

If you're saving for several things at once (emergency fund, holiday, gifts), set up separate orders to separate accounts or "goals" in your app. Seeing "Holiday: PLN 2,400 of 4,000" is far more motivating than one big unnamed number.

How much to save?

  • Start with an amount you'll barely notice – even 5% of your pay or PLN 100. Small and regular beats big for two months and then nothing.
  • Raise it every few months – e.g. by PLN 50, or with every pay rise.
  • Keep a buffer in your current account, so the automatic transfer doesn't push you into overdraft when something unexpected comes up.

Common mistakes

  • Transfer date before payday – the money isn't there yet and the transfer fails.
  • An over-ambitious amount – after a month you move it back and get discouraged.
  • Savings in the same account as spending money – they just melt away.

In Money Trackrr you can add a goal deposit as a fixed payment – the app will show when you'll reach your goal at that pace.

In short

  • Set up a standing order to savings the day after payday.
  • Add round-ups if your bank offers them.
  • Start small and increase the amount every few months.
