Emergency fund – how much to save?

25 October 2026·2 min read·Money Trackrr editorial team

A broken washing machine, a trip to the mechanic or a sudden job loss – an emergency fund turns that into a problem instead of a disaster. Here is how big it should be, where to keep it and how to build it step by step, even on a modest income.

An emergency fund is money set aside only for unexpected situations. Not for holidays, not for a new phone – only for things you can't plan. With it, you don't have to take a payday loan or max out a card when something goes wrong.

How big should it be?

The most common rule: 3–6 months of your living costs. Note – costs, not income. Count what you need to spend to get by: housing, bills, food, commuting, loan payments.

Example: if your basic costs are PLN 3,500 a month, your fund should be roughly PLN 10,500 to PLN 21,000.

Aim closer to 3 months if:

  • you have a stable employment contract,
  • there are two independent incomes in your household.

Aim closer to 6 months (or more) if:

  • you're freelancing or running a business,
  • your income is irregular,
  • you support a family on one salary.

Where to start

PLN 10–20 thousand can feel overwhelming. So build it in stages:

  1. Mini fund: PLN 1,000–2,000. Covers most small breakdowns and gives you a first breather.
  2. One month of costs. This is where real peace of mind begins.
  3. Three months. Solid protection for most people.
  4. Your target amount – based on your situation.

The simplest method: a standing order to a separate account right after payday, even if it's only PLN 100–200.

Where to keep it

Your emergency fund must be safe and available within 1–2 days. Good options:

  • a savings account – interest plus access at any time,
  • short term deposits – for the part you're unlikely to need immediately.

Shares, crypto and other investments are not suitable – their value can drop exactly when you need the money.

When to use it

Ask yourself three questions: Is it unexpected? Is it necessary? Is it urgent? If the answer to all three is yes, use the fund without guilt. Afterwards, simply rebuild it.

In short

  • Emergency fund = 3–6 months of living costs, only for the unexpected.
  • Build it in stages: first PLN 1,000–2,000, then one month, then three.
  • Keep it safe and within reach – in a savings account, not in investments.
