About us

Money Trackrr is a small, independent project: a free expense and budget tracker, plus a site that translates financial news into what it means for your wallet.

The tool

The tracker runs in your browser and offline, with no account. Your data stays on your device – we never send it to a server or share it with advertisers. You can install it like an app on your phone or computer.

How our articles are made

  • We always cite sources. Below every article you will find links to the material we rely on: public institutions, news agencies and financial outlets.
  • Facts as of the publication date. Numbers (prices, exchange rates, interest rates) come with a date – things may change later.
  • What it means for you. Every article ends with a practical explanation of how the news affects a household budget.
  • No investment advice. Our content is for information and education only. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
  • AI tools. We use AI tools to help with research and editing. Facts are checked against the sources listed below each article.
  • Corrections. If you spot a mistake, tell us – we will fix it and update the date.

Funding

The project is free. It is supported by Google ads shown only in articles (never in the tracker itself) and by voluntary support on Buy me a coffee.
