The 50/30/20 rule in practice
The simplest way to split your pay: half on needs, 30% on wants, 20% on savings. Here is how to apply it to your own salary and what to do when the numbers don't add up.
---
The 50/30/20 rule is a simple split of the money you take home each month. No spreadsheets with dozens of categories – just three buckets.

## Three buckets

- **50% – needs.** Things that are hard to live without: rent or mortgage, bills, groceries, commuting, medicine, minimum debt payments.
- **30% – wants.** Everything that makes life nicer but that you could live without: restaurants, takeaway, extra clothes, going out, streaming, hobbies, holidays.
- **20% – the future.** Savings, an emergency fund, extra debt repayments, investing.

## An example with numbers

You earn PLN 5,000 net. Following the rule:

- PLN 2,500 for needs,
- PLN 1,500 for wants,
- PLN 1,000 for savings and paying down debt.

Ideally that PLN 1,000 leaves your account **right after payday** – with a standing order to a separate account. Then you don't have to remember to save; you spend what's left.

## What if needs take more than 50%?

This is common – especially if you rent in a big city or are early in your career. There is nothing wrong with that. You can adjust the rule:

- **60/20/20** – when housing costs alone are high,
- **70/20/10** – for a tougher period, as long as savings don't drop to zero,
- **50/20/30** – when you're paying off expensive debt and want to do it faster.

The numbers are a guide, not a verdict. What matters is that some percentage always goes to the future – even if it's 5%.

## Getting started in 15 minutes

1. Check how much actually lands in your account each month (net, after all deductions).
2. Go through last month's spending and assign it to the three buckets.
3. Compare with the rule – where are you most "over"?
4. Set up a standing order to savings on payday.
5. After a month, check how it went and adjust the proportions.

In Money Trackrr you can set budgets for categories and a savings goal – so you always see how much is left in each bucket.

## In short

- 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% future – based on your net pay.
- Move savings at the start of the month, not "from whatever is left".
- Adapt the proportions to your situation – just make sure something always goes to the future.
